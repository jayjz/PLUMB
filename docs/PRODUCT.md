# Product thesis and commercial gates

## Candidate user and job
Small contractor or estimator with fragmented calls, site notes, photos, and proposal revisions. Job to be done: **Before sending a proposal, identify unresolved mismatches and omissions that could force repricing, rework, or awkward customer clarification.**

## Proposed wedge
A **pre-send scope check**, not automatic estimating. Inputs: field-visit transcript, customer amendments, scope draft. Outputs: evidence-grounded questions for human review. Preserve ambiguity and version precedence.

## Porter / JTBD discipline
Rivalry and substitutes are substantial: proposal tools, estimating suites, checklists, telephone calls and trained estimators. Differentiation must be verified on **cross-source discrepancies + credible citations + low false-alarm burden**, not simply an LLM summary. Competitors may already cover portions of this. These are hypotheses; do not claim market whitespace.

## Commercial tests (not yet performed)
1. Ten discovery interviews about actual delayed/revised estimates; record negative responses too.
2. Three voluntary, consented anonymized job-record trials with a known human review outcome.
3. Compare to contractor's current review process for missed issues, review time, and false alarms.
4. Ask about a paid continuation before committing to broad integrations.

## Not in scope for EXP-001
Live customer data, recordings, photos, LiDAR, estimation/pricing, CRM/FSM integration, scheduling, agent autonomy, deployment UI, paid subscriptions.

## Safety and privacy
Human review is mandatory. Do not declare code compliance, permit requirements, structural suitability, or job costs. Respect consent, retention, redaction and applicable laws before real recordings.
